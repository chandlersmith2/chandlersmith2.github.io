Welcome. I am a Hale Visiting Assistant Professor in the <a href="https://math.gatech.edu/">School of Mathematics at Georgia Tech</a>, working with <a href="https://dieci.math.gatech.edu/">Luca Dieci</a> and <a href="https://kang.math.gatech.edu/">Sung Ha Kang</a>. I completed my PhD at Tufts University with <a href="https://abiy-tasissa.github.io/">Abiy Tasissa</a>. My research interests include optimization, inverse problems, and their connections to geometry.
