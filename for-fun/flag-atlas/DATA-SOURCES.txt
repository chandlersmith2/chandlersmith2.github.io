Flag Atlas data sources

Country and territory names, regions and UN membership: mledoze/countries
https://github.com/mledoze/countries
Original database licensed under Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0:
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
The derived database in countries.json is made available under the same ODbL 1.0 license.

Scope: all 249 ISO 3166-1 entries, plus Kosovo (XK), 250 entries total.
Core deck: 193 UN member states plus the Holy See (Vatican City) and Palestine, 195 entries.
The expanded deck includes territories, dependencies and disputed entities. Inclusion does not express a position on sovereignty.
Antarctic and some uninhabited-territory flags are representative designs, not necessarily official sovereign-state flags.

Flag images: FlagCDN, https://flagcdn.com/ (https://flagpedia.net/download/api).
Downloaded November 2, 2026. Flag illustrations may follow the provider's conventions for territories and disputed entities.
